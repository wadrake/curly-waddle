Thrill Rides at Six Flags Over Georgia

Acrophobia

Height: 200 ft | Drop: 161 ft | Speed: 50 mph

Acrophobia is the world’s first floorless, tilting stand-up drop tower. Riders are lifted 20 stories high before tilting forward and dropping at 50 mph. A must for thrill-seekers!

Goliath

Height: 200 ft | Speed: 70 mph | Track Length: 4,480 ft

This hyper coaster offers massive drops, intense airtime hills, and high-speed turns. It's one of the tallest and fastest rides in the Southeast.

Batman: The Ride

Type: Inverted Coaster | Speed: 50 mph

Feel like the Dark Knight as you zoom through twists, loops, and zero-g rolls while suspended from an overhead track.

THE RIDDLER Mind Bender

Type: Steel Looper | Speed: 50 mph

A classic looping coaster that delivers smooth turns, double loops, and riverside views. Recently refurbished for a new generation of fans.

Dare Devil Dive

Drop Angle: 95 degrees | Type: Euro-Fighter

This ride features a vertical lift followed by a beyond-vertical drop and tight inversions, all packed into a compact track layout.

Blue Hawk

Height: 122 ft | Speed: 52 mph | Inversions: 5

Originally opened as Ninja, Blue Hawk is a smooth, steel twister coaster with stunning views over a lake. After major renovations in 2016, the ride now features soft vest restraints and a revamped track. It includes 5 inversions such as a butterfly, reverse sidewinder, and double corkscrew.

Georgia Gold Rusher

Height: 144 ft | Speed: 60 mph | Track Length: 590 ft

Experience the thrill of the all-new Georgia Gold Rusher, a groundbreaking coaster featuring free-spinning gondolas on a U-shaped track. Inspired by Georgia's 19th-century gold rush, this ride launches you up to 144 feet at speeds reaching 60 mph, delivering a unique adventure with every ride.

Georgia Scorcher

Height: 107 ft | Speed: 54 mph | Inversions: 2

Georgia Scorcher is a steel stand-up roller coaster located at Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia. Manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard, it opened on May 8, 1999, and was the last stand-up coaster installation built for almost 24 years until 2023. The ride features a 107-foot lift hill, a 101-foot drop, and reaches speeds up to 54 mph. Riders experience two inversions: an 81-foot vertical loop and a corkscrew. The coaster's layout includes a unique treble clef-shaped element and a ground-level 270-degree helix. In 2019, Georgia Scorcher was repainted with a cherry red track and dark gray supports.

JUSTICE LEAGUE: Battle for Metropolis

Type: Interactive 4D Dark Ride | Duration: ~3:45 min

Join the Justice League Reserve Team in a thrilling mission to save Metropolis! This cutting-edge, fully immersive dark ride combines motion-based vehicles, 3D screens, animatronics, and special effects. Armed with an EMP blaster, you'll battle alongside Superman, Batman, and Wonder Woman against Lex Luthor and The Joker in an epic fight to rescue the captured heroes and restore peace to the city.

Pandemonium

Height: 147 ft | Speed: 70 mph | Capacity: 40 riders

Pandemonium is a colossal pendulum ride located in the Screampunk District of Six Flags Over Georgia. Manufactured by Zamperla, this Giant Discovery model swings riders up to 147 feet in the air while rotating counterclockwise, reaching speeds of 70 mph. Opened on May 24, 2019, Pandemonium offers an exhilarating experience of weightlessness and high-flying thrills, making it one of the park's most intense attractions.

Twisted Cyclone

Height: 100 ft | Speed: 50 mph | Inversions: 3

Twisted Cyclone is a hybrid roller coaster located at Six Flags Over Georgia, combining a classic wooden structure with a modern steel track. Designed by Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC), the ride features a 100-foot drop, speeds of up to 50 mph, and three thrilling inversions. Since its debut on May 25, 2018, Twisted Cyclone has become a fan-favorite for its smooth ride, airtime moments, and unique overbanked turns that offer an exhilarating and modern take on a historic coaster.

SkyScreamer

Height: 242 ft | Speed: 40 mph | Ride Type: Swing Tower

SkyScreamer is a towering swing ride located at Six Flags Over Georgia, offering breathtaking panoramic views from 242 feet in the air. Manufactured by Funtime, this swing tower ride lifts riders high above the park before spinning them at speeds up to 40 mph in open-air seats. Since its opening in 2013, SkyScreamer has become a staple thrill attraction for guests seeking both excitement and a bird’s-eye view of the park’s surroundings.

Superman: Ultimate Flight

Height: 106 ft | Speed: 60 mph | Inversions: 2

Superman: Ultimate Flight is a thrilling flying roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia, designed by Bolliger & Mabillard. This coaster simulates the sensation of flight, positioning riders face-down as they soar through high-speed drops, sharp turns, and the signature pretzel loop. Reaching a height of 106 feet and speeds of 60 mph, Superman: Ultimate Flight delivers a heroic experience that combines grace and adrenaline, capturing the essence of Superman’s iconic aerial maneuvers.

The Great American Scream Machine

Height: 105 ft | Speed: 57 mph | Inversions: 0

The Great American Scream Machine is a classic wooden roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia, known for its traditional out-and-back layout and intense airtime hills. Opened on March 31, 1973, it was once the tallest and fastest wooden coaster in the world. With a height of 105 feet and a top speed of 57 mph, this ride offers a nostalgic thrill with powerful drops and rattling turns that pay homage to the golden age of wooden coasters.
